QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

Good to know.

Can I start with one barber and upgrade later?

Yes. Start with Solo and discuss an upgrade as your team grows.

Are WhatsApp and SMS included?

Messaging is usage-based and quoted separately.

Does BarberPro file GST returns?

BarberPro is intended to help prepare invoice and sales records for review and export. It does not file GST returns on your behalf. Confirm tax treatment and return formats with your accountant.

Can I manage more than one outlet?

Enterprise is designed for multi-outlet businesses. Outlet limits and capabilities are confirmed during onboarding.
